# Media Bible — Concept, Research & Idea Shortlist
*Working name. Draft v0.1 — 2026-10-31*

---

## 1. The one-line pitch

**An agentic "narrative war room" for political and public-facing communicators: it listens to the public conversation, maps the narratives forming in it, tests messages on a synthetic electorate before they go out, drafts content in the leader's own voice, and learns from every post — with a human approving every step and integrity built in.**

Short version for the slide: *From noise → narrative → message → movement.*

---

## 2. What the market already has (research summary)

| Category | Who is doing it | What they lack (our gap) |
|---|---|---|
| **Social listening dashboards** | Brandwatch/Talkwalker-type tools, DIRECTV's new AI political ad platform (LLM + social + donation + voting signals) | They report *what happened*. They don't tell you *what to do next*, and they don't act. Consultants say many listening tools are "poor and prone to error". |
| **AI content generation for campaigns** | Quiller (fundraising emails), Chorus AI, BattlegroundAI (ad generation) | Generation with no listening, testing or learning loop. Generic voice. |
| **AI-answer visibility** | CampSight (Run for Something): tracks what ChatGPT-style chatbots say about a candidate, from 300k+ chatbot conversations | A single feature. Nobody combines it with social listening. |
| **Real-time counter-narrative bots** | LogiVote ("digital fighters"), Convos, Vector Political (AI voter-conversation agents) | Reputational and legal risk: hidden bots, astroturf. **This is the space we explicitly do *not* go into.** |
| **Synthetic focus groups** | Electric Twin, Artificial Societies (audiences that influence each other), GWI, Blend360, Kumkuat (corporate comms) | Commercial or brand focus. Critics flag "hallucinated empathy": personas need grounding in real data and calibration. |
| **Virality predictors** | Higgsfield Virality Predictor, Hook IQ, Go Viral | Clip-level scoring for creators only. No political or audience context. |

**What the research shows:**
1. The tools are **fragmented**. A comms team today stitches together 4–6 tools plus an agency plus gut feeling. Nobody runs the full loop *listen → understand → decide → create → test → publish → learn*. **That loop is where agentic workflows come in, and it's our speciality.**
2. Analysts (Tech Policy Press) argue the biggest political impact of generative AI is **listening, not deepfakes**. AI can run open-ended "focus groups at the scale of a whole population". One candidate paid $15k for a two-question poll.
3. **Regulation is now live**. EU AI Act Art. 50 (AI-content disclosure, Aug 2026) and the EU TTPA (political ad transparency, influencer disclosure, labelling of synthetic media) are in force. A compliance-by-design product is a **selling point**, not a burden.
4. A new channel: **what AI chatbots say about you** is becoming as important as what the press says.

## 3. Full brainstorm (everything on the table)

**A. Listening & understanding**
1. **Omni-Listener**: agents ingest X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube comments, Telegram channels, news, podcasts (via transcription) and *AI chatbot answers*. Multilingual, slang- and dialect-aware.
2. **Narrative Radar**: clusters millions of posts into *narratives* (not keywords). Each narrative gets a lifecycle stage (born → accelerating → peaking → fading), sentiment, emotional tone and the people driving it.
3. **Influence Graph**: who carries a narrative. Hubs, bridges between communities, journalists, micro-influencers.
4. **Constituency Map**: geo heatmap of issues by region or district ("what does Tabriz care about this week vs. Shiraz?").
5. **Coordinated-behaviour detector**: flags bot swarms and astroturf *against* you, so you don't overreact to fake outrage.
6. **AI-Mirror**: what ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and others say about the leader, which sources they cite, and how to correct misinformation at the source.

**B. Foresight & decision**
7. **Early-Warning System**: predicts which narratives will cross into mainstream in the next 6–48 hours. "Crisis weather forecast."
8. **Synthetic Electorate (digital-twin audiences)**: personas *grounded in real comments, polls and demographics*. Message variants are tested before publishing, with predicted reactions per segment and a calibration score against past real outcomes.
9. **Strategy Council**: several specialist agents (Strategist, Devil's Advocate, Legal/Integrity, Data Analyst) *debate* a decision and present the options with trade-offs to the human. The AI behaviour is visible and explainable.
10. **Opponent Lens**: tracks rivals' public statements, positioning shifts and contradictions (public data only).
11. **Debate & Interview Simulator**: an agent plays a hostile journalist trained on that journalist's real past questions.

**C. Creation & distribution**
12. **Voice DNA**: learns the leader's real rhetorical style (vocabulary, rhythm, values) from speeches and posts. Every draft "sounds like them" and stays consistent with past positions.
13. **Creative Studio**: turns one idea into a reel script, a carousel, an X thread, a Telegram post, a speech paragraph and talking points, each adapted per platform.
14. **Hook Lab + Virality Forecast**: generates 20 hooks, ranks them by predicted retention on each platform and on each audience segment.
15. **Rapid Response Pipeline**: a narrative spikes, and within minutes the system produces a fact-checked response draft with sources, ready for one-click approval.
16. **Content Calendar Autopilot**: plans the week around events, anniversaries and predicted narrative windows.

**D. Learning & governance**
17. **Learning Loop / Playbook memory**: after publishing, it attributes what worked and why, then updates the strategy playbook. The system gets smarter every week.
18. **Morning Brief**: a 3-minute personalised daily briefing (audio or video) for the leader. "Here's your day in narratives."
19. **Integrity Layer**: human-in-the-loop approval on everything, C2PA provenance and AI-disclosure labels, no fake accounts, no undisclosed bots, no deepfakes of real people, full audit log, EU AI Act and TTPA compliance reports.
20. **Team War Room**: roles (leader, comms director, social team, legal) with approval chains and mobile alerts.

| # | Feature | Why it's in the video |
|---|---|---|

## 7. Decisions (2026-10-31) & Iran localisation

**Decided:** Iranian politicians and media are the audience · 6-feature shortlist approved · ~3 min · neutral product name.

**Localisation changes**
- **Language:** Persian VO, RTL Persian UI (Vazirmatn font), Persian numerals. English only for small technical labels if needed.
- **Platforms in the UI:** Instagram, Telegram, X, Eitaa, Rubika, Bale, Aparat, news sites, plus "what AI chatbots say".
- **Demo scenario:** use a civic, low-sensitivity issue instead of a fuel-price rumour (too charged locally). Proposal: **Tehran air pollution / school closures**, with **summer power outages (خاموشی)** as the backup. A rumour spreads on Telegram, Radar catches it, Council debates, Synthetic Electorate tests three responses, Studio produces a reel and a Telegram post.
- **Integrity Layer reframed:** less about EU law, more about trust: no fake accounts, no bots posing as people, fact-checked sources, human approval, a full audit trail, and detection of coordinated campaigns *against* the client.
- **Fictional leader persona:** use a fictional mayor or MP (e.g. "a city council member") with a generated, clearly fictional portrait. No real politician.

**Neutral name options**
| Name | Meaning | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Ravi / راوی** | "narrator": the one who tells the story | Fits "narrative" perfectly; literary and memorable |
| **Nabz / نبض** | "pulse" (of society) | Short, listening-focused |
| **Agora** | public square | International, works in both languages |
| **Pishkhan / پیشخوان** | "front desk / newsstand" | Familiar, media-flavoured |
