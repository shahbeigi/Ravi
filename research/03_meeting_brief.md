# Meeting brief: commercial Ravi

2026-11-02 · Prepared for the media-partner discussion.

## Opening proposition

**We can build a platform that turns a brand's campaign target into a media plan, coordinates execution across publishers and social platforms, and shows evidence of what the budget delivered. AI makes the planning, production, monitoring, and reporting faster; our media network and measurement discipline make the outcome credible.**

Persian version:

«راوی هدف و بودجهٔ برند را به یک برنامهٔ رسانه‌ای قابل اجرا تبدیل می‌کند؛ از انتخاب رسانه و آماده‌سازی محتوا تا پیگیری انتشار، سنجش نتیجه و ارائهٔ شواهد. هوش مصنوعی سرعت و بهره‌وری تیم را بالا می‌برد و همکاری با رسانه‌ها، امکان اجرای واقعی کمپین را فراهم می‌کند.»

Your strongest contribution is brand clients and sales relationships. Our strongest contribution is agentic software and execution automation. **Distribution access and measurement evidence are the third part we must secure together.**

## The proposed initial offer

Sell a managed campaign package with separate creative/operations fees and media spend. Begin with a narrow vertical chosen from the partner's actual pipeline, and two or three channels selected for supplier access and evidence quality.

For consumer brands, Instagram + Telegram + one validated domestic channel is a candidate, not a predetermined requirement. For industrial clients, specialist publishers, Telegram communities, Aparat explainers, and lead capture may be more useful than broad consumer reach.

Start with target-based planning and reporting. Offer binding delivery commitments only on specific inventory with compatible supplier commitments, measurable definitions, and capped remedies. Organic virality, positive sentiment, exact sales, and universal unique reach are not launch guarantees.

## What the competitive research means

Jaryan already advertises multi-platform local campaigns; Yektanet and Triboon cover substantial adjacent distribution needs. Sprinklr, Smartly, and CreatorIQ demonstrate international precedents for orchestration, creative optimization, and creator measurement. We should demonstrate measurable operational improvement rather than claim the category is empty. See the linked evidence in [the research report](01_research_and_feasibility.md).

KhabarOnline's own public material describes commercial placements and advertising-derived revenue. We can work with such publishers as supply partners. We cannot replace their audience or credibility merely by generating more content.

## A useful meeting demonstration

Use a fictional appliance brand launching a new product. Label all mock numbers as illustrative.

1. Enter product facts, audience, budget ceiling, deadline, and a primary outcome.
2. Ravi asks for missing metric definitions and proposes three plans from a sample supplier catalogue.
3. Show line-item price, forecast range, evidence method, and cancellation terms.
4. Generate platform-specific drafts from the same approved product facts.
5. Approve the exact assets and placements.
6. Show a missed publication and a proposed replacement using uncommitted budget.
7. Open the evidence behind each result, then the final financial reconciliation.

The strongest moment is an actionable, correctly costed recovery plan with traceable evidence. Avoid fabricated “AI predicted 94% virality” screens.

## Pilot proposal

Proposed scope: three paying brands, six campaigns in one initial vertical, roughly 10–20 vetted suppliers, and 2–3 supported channels. These numbers are design choices, not statistical proof. Run manual and automated operations together so failures reveal process requirements before heavy engineering.

Measure:

- Operator minutes per campaign and proposal turnaround against comparable historical work.
- Percentage of billed placements with acceptable evidence and on-time publication.
- Actual delivered outcomes versus agreed estimates, using platform-specific definitions.
- Contribution margin after labor, media, generation, revisions, refunds, and make-goods.
- Client willingness to renew at a price that covers the real operating work.

Suggested go/no-go gates, to agree before starting:

- Three clients pay, rather than merely express interest.
- At least two viable supply routes and enough backup inventory for the pilot.
- Every billed placement meets its agreed evidence standard; exceptions are explicitly withheld or resolved.
- No unapproved spend or publication.
- Meaningful operator-time reduction against a documented baseline; 30% can be a target, not a promised result.
- Positive campaign contribution after allocating operator time and delivery remedies.
- At least two pilot clients request a paid continuation. This is a directional signal from a small sample, not product-market-fit proof.

## Economics: measure the whole campaign

Use this model before setting a price:

`Campaign contribution = client receipts − media costs − production costs − operator labor − AI/hosting/data allocation − payment costs − refunds/make-goods − partner commissions`

Media pass-through is not automatically software revenue. Whether revenue is booked gross or net depends on principal/agent arrangements and accounting advice. Track media volume, service revenue, and contribution separately in internal dashboards.

Illustrative stress test, **not Iranian market pricing**, all amounts in millions of toman:

| Item | Base scenario |
|---|---:|
| Client campaign payment | 100 |
| Supplier media cost | 65 |
| Creative production and operating labor | 12 |
| AI, hosting, data, payment allocation | 3 |
| Partner commission | 8 |
| Expected delivery remedy cost | 5 |
| Contribution before fixed company costs and taxes | 7 |

If remedy costs rise from 5 to 12, contribution becomes zero. If media costs increase 10% while the client price stays fixed, contribution falls to 0.5. If both occur, it becomes −6.5. Cheap AI does not protect a poorly priced distribution commitment.

Distinguish cash reserved for possible remedies from realized expense; the table uses expected cost for planning. Also model cash timing: clients should fund media before non-refundable publisher commitments, unless separately approved credit economics support another arrangement.

Do not reuse the earlier GPU-per-customer or model-token estimates for this product. New cost drivers are media buying, operations, creative revision, evidence collection, and shortfalls. Collect actual campaign logs before proposing annual pricing.

## Partnership questions to settle

1. Which five brands can the partner realistically introduce now, and who signs their budgets?
2. What did their last three campaigns buy, cost, deliver, and report? Can we inspect anonymized evidence?
3. Are customers asking for views, qualified attention, leads, sales, or reputation—and which are they prepared to pay for?
4. Which publishers and creators can supply placements, at what rates, with what analytics access?
5. Who owns client relationships, software IP, creative assets, and permissioned learning data?
6. Who operates campaigns, handles revisions, approves public content, and resolves disputes?
7. Who is merchant of record, collects payments, bears nonpayment, and funds shortfalls?
8. How are media spend, service fees, commissions, and profit shares separated?
9. Are any exclusivity terms narrow, time-bound, and conditional on minimum paid business?
10. What does success after the first six campaigns look like, and what would make us stop?

Avoid committing to a permanent joint venture or broad exclusivity before the pilot resolves these questions. This is a commercial recommendation, not legal drafting.

## Proposed 60-minute agenda

| Minutes | Topic | Desired output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Review real client demand and one past campaign | Buyer and primary problem |
| 10–20 | Agree measurable outcomes and evidence | Shared definition of delivery |
| 20–30 | Review available supply and buying model | Access gaps and responsible owner |
| 30–40 | Walk through campaign workflow | Product scope and operational ownership |
| 40–50 | Discuss pilot economics and risk allocation | Commercial pilot outline |
| 50–60 | Commit next actions | Named owners, source materials, dates |

Recommended decision: authorize a time-boxed discovery and paid pilot, contingent on real supplier quotes and account-access checks. The meeting should establish whether demand, distribution, and evidence can be assembled into a profitable service.
